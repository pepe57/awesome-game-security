# T-1

C++ **shellcode loader** that uses machine-learning–assisted **virtual-machine detection**. Python scripts with scikit-learn train and tune a decision-tree classifier; the resulting rules are translated into native C++ environment checks. Based on the detection outcome, the sample can branch to shellcode execution or self-removal—typical sandbox-awareness behavior studied in malware and anti-analysis research. Intended for research on automation-assisted malware analysis and VM/sandbox evasion techniques. (source: wiki/sources/descriptions/0xTriboulet__T-1.md)

Complements modular anti-analysis testers such as [[pafish]] and [[al-khaser]], embeddable VM fingerprinting such as [[compact-vm-detector]], and sandbox demos such as [[anti-sandbox]] and [[wsb-detect]].
